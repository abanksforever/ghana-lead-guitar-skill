# Example 3: Coaching report from a recorded lead take

**Request:**

> I recorded myself playing a lead line in Bb at 100 bpm (my_lead_take.wav). Be honest, what should I work on to sound like a proper church lead guitarist?

**Response from the skill:**

I measured your take with the analyser in Bb at 100 BPM on an 8th-note grid. There's no click in the file, so these numbers show how steady you are, not whether you rush or drag against a band.

**What's working:** your time is solid. All 10 notes were within 25 ms of the grid and the spread was 12 ms, which is tight. Your line also moves by step and resolves home to Bb, which is real melodic sense.

**Priority 1: One note out of tune** (evidence: the G was +37 cents sharp, all other notes within 5 cents)
A single sharp note usually means you're pressing too hard or pulling the string sideways. It can also be a bend that went too far.
- **Drill:** play the G (and every note around it) over a Bb drone, holding each note for 4 beats. Use the lightest pressure that still rings clean.
- **Tempo and time:** 60 BPM, 5 minutes a day.
- **Pass when:** a tuner app shows ±10 cents on every held note.

**Priority 2: An E natural in a Bb line** (evidence: 1 out-of-key note, E4, between F and D)
In Bb the 4th is Eb (sol-fa f). E natural (fi) only works as a quick chromatic passing note, and here it lands on a beat, so it sounds like a mistake.
- **Drill:** sing the line in sol-fa before you play it. Then play the Bb major scale and the Bb diatonic sixths ladder (`gtr.py doublestops Bb --strings Ge`).
- **Tempo and time:** 70 BPM, 10 minutes a day.
- **Pass when:** you play the line 5 times in a row with Eb.

**Priority 3: Leave more space to answer the singer** (evidence: notes sounding 81% of the time, with no rests)
A church lead line breathes. Play in the gaps after the singer's lines, not on top of them.
- **Drill:** loop a 2-bar vocal phrase and answer it with 1 bar, then rest a full bar.
- **Tempo and time:** song tempo, 10 minutes a day.
- **Pass when:** your recording is under 60% sounding and still sounds like music.

**This week (25 minutes a day)**
- **Mon–Tue:** 5 minutes intonation drone, 10 minutes the Bb scale in sol-fa, 10 minutes the sixths ladder.
- **Wed–Thu:** 5 minutes drone, then 20 minutes call and response. Use lick 1 from the library, transposed to Bb with capo 3.
- **Fri:** record the same line again over a click at 100 BPM. Rerun the analysis and compare.
- **Sat:** play the line with the praise team's track and focus only on space.

**Next listen:** Kojo Antwi records, for how the guitar answers in the gaps, and Joe Mettle live worship sets, for restraint and tone.

To get a rushing/dragging verdict next time, record over a click or backing track that's audible in the file.
