# Example 2: Three licks for a highlife praise song in Ab

**Request:**

> Our MD says we're doing a highlife-style praise in Ab, it goes 1 4 1 5 the whole time. I'm intermediate on electric. Give me an intro lick, a lick to answer the singer, and an ending.

**Response from the skill:**

**Ab plan:** put the capo on fret 1 and play G shapes. That makes 1 4 1 5 = G C G D in shapes (Ab Db Ab Eb in concert). In sol-fa, do = Ab. Every tab below is in capo-relative frets and was checked with `gtr.py check --key Ab --capo 1` against the concert chords.

**1. Intro: pentatonic call (over 1 to 4)**
```
  Ab                   Db
e|--------7---10-7----|-----------------|
B|---8-10----------10-|-8---5-----------|
G|--------------------|---------9---5---|
D|--------------------|-----------------|
A|--------------------|-----------------|
E|--------------------|-----------------|
   1 & 2  & 3 &  4 &    1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```
Sol-fa: d r m s m r | d l l f. Pick it softly and slide into the e7. In bar 2 the line falls down the 4 chord and lands on its root (G5). Practice it at 70 BPM, then work up to the band's tempo. The check found 0 out-of-key notes.

**2. Vocal answer: sixths into the 4**
```
  Ab                  Db
e|-------------------|------------------|
B|---8---10-12-------|-10---8---7---5---|
G|-------------------|------------------|
D|---9---10-12-------|-10---9---7---5---|
A|-------------------|------------------|
E|-------------------|------------------|
   1 & 2 &  3  & 4 &   1  & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```
Play it only in the gap after the singer's line. Start on the "&" so you don't step on their last word. Use two fingers and slide between the pairs. The final pair sits on the 5th and 3rd of Db. Practice at 70 BPM, target 110. The check found 0 out-of-key notes.

**3. Ending: thirds walk-down, 5 to 1**
```
  Eb                Ab
e|-----------------|-----------------|
B|-7---5---3---1---|-0---------------|
G|-7---5---4---2---|-0---------------|
D|-----------------|-----------------|
A|-----------------|-----------------|
E|-----------------|-----------------|
   1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &   1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```
Thirds on the G and B strings walk down the scale (s-t, f-l, m-s, r-f) and settle on d-m, the open strings under the capo. Let them ring with a little vibrato while the band holds the last chord. Practice at 60 BPM so every pair is clean. The check found 0 out-of-key notes.

**Tone:** clean, neck pickup, a touch of chorus for the highlife colour.
**Next step:** play all three licks with the capo off in G. Then play them with the capo on fret 3 (Bb) so they're ready when the MD changes key.
